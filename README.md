# 🌌 Aphelion

**Plot your orbit. Fly the transfer. Explore another world.**

Aphelion is an interactive spaceflight and planetary exploration sandbox built using **Grok** for the **BDBI x SpaceXSI Grokathon**.

Begin in Earth orbit aboard **Vesper**, shape your trajectory, and follow a Keplerian path to a planet, a Kuiper-belt world, or the Sun. Reach your destination, explore its globe, and step onto the surface.

## 🚀 Try Aphelion here: https://branch-arch-fleet-zinc.vercel.app/

## 🛰️ Shape Your Journey

Take control of your orbit through impulse burns and interactive sliders.

- **Prograde, normal, and radial burns** let you change your trajectory.
- Apply burns at your **current position, periapsis, apoapsis, or an orbital node**.
- Adjust **apoapsis, periapsis, and inclination** with the shape sliders.
- Preview your next orbit before committing—the **pale line** shows the path you are about to fly.

## 🎯 Find Your Landing

Fly the approach yourself or let **Auto-land** handle the journey.

Auto-land waits for the launch window, coasts through the transfer, and matches the target’s motion. If you arrive manually, **Land now** becomes available when you are close enough and moving slowly enough relative to your destination.

## 🪐 Discover Each World

On arrival, explore a rotating globe whose rendering draws on the body’s **mass, radius, distance, and atmosphere**.

Discover cloud bands, surface heat maps, and twilight scattering along the limb. Switch between three views:

| View | Explore |
|---|---|
| 🎨 **Albedo** | Surface reflectivity |
| 🌐 **Composite** | The world’s combined appearance |
| 🌡️ **Heat** | Surface temperature patterns |

## 👨‍🚀 Step Onto the Surface

Leave the orbital view behind and explore on foot. Surface markers and the **codex** explain the features around you.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **W / S** | Move forward / backward |
| **A / D** | Turn left / right |
| **Space** | Jump |
| **Mobile stick** | Move and turn on a phone |

## 🔭 Explore Beyond the Kuiper Belt

Catalog worlds beyond the Kuiper belt open in the same globe explorer using **published data**.

These distant worlds are available for direct exploration through the catalog; they are **not destinations for a simulated ballistic flight**.

## 🛠️ Built for the Grokathon

Created using **Grok** for the **BDBI x SpaceXSI Grokathon**, Aphelion brings orbital mechanics, planetary visualization, and surface exploration into one playable journey.
