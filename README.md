# Aphelion is ready. 

# My project for the BDBI x SpaceXSI Grokathon, built using Grok.

## You start in Earth orbit aboard Vesper and fly a real Keplerian path to a planet, a Kuiper-belt world, or the Sun.

### Maneuver with prograde, normal, and radial burns at your current point, periapsis, apoapsis, or a node. The shape sliders set apoapsis, periapsis, and inclination, and the pale line is the orbit you are about to fly.
### Auto-land waits out the launch window, coasts the transfer, and matches the target. If you arrive on your own, Land now appears when you are close and slow enough.
### On arrival you get a rotating globe built from that body's mass, radius, distance, and atmosphere — cloud bands, a heat map, and twilight scattering along the limb. Albedo, composite, and heat are separate views.
### Walk the surface with W and S, turn with A and D (A is left), and jump with Space. On a phone, use the stick. Markers and the codex explain what you are standing on. Catalog worlds past the Kuiper belt open the same globe from published data. They are not flown as a ballistic trip.
